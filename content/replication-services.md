# Research Replication Services

We run bespoke replication studies of published research and adapt them to your data. Each replication is delivered as a set of signed, citable nanopublications — verifiable, reusable, and built on the open [Science Live](https://sciencelive4all.org) platform.

## Who this is for

- **Research organizations** validating prior work before building on it
- **Industry R&D teams** assessing whether a published method holds up on their data
- **Funders** commissioning independent verification of supported work
- **Policy and standards bodies** that need evidence-grade replication

## What's included

- Bespoke replication of your chosen study
- Adaptation to your data and workflows
- Signed, citable nanopublications of the results
- Brief written report with source code and reproducible environment
- Direct support from the team throughout the engagement

## Pricing

**€2,000–€10,000 per replication.**

The final price depends on data complexity and the algorithm involved. We agree a fixed quote within that range up front, before any work begins.

## How we work

1. **Scope** — We discuss the study, your data, and what "replicated" means for your purposes.
2. **Quote** — A fixed price within the €2,000–€10,000 range, agreed in writing up front.
3. **Replicate** — We run the study using the open Science Live platform.
4. **Deliver** — Signed nanopublications, written report, source code, and reproducible environment.
